Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is my data?

We utilize military-grade AES-256 encryption. Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device and remains encrypted on our servers. We employ strict access controls, and we strictly adhere to the policy that we never sell your data to third parties. Read the full bank-level security breakdown →

Can I share my vault with multiple family members?

Yes. Depending on your pricing tier, you can invite trusted family members or legal delegates. You maintain full control over who has access, and you can specify exactly which documents or folders each person is allowed to view.

What happens if I lose my password?

For security reasons, we do not store your master password. Upon signup, you will be provided with an Emergency Recovery Kit containing a unique cryptographic key. It is crucial to store this kit in a safe physical location.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from the Basic to Premium vault at any time right from your dashboard dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades will take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
